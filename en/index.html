How it started

I'm Gustave. It started with a holiday home in Sicily that I look after: its welcome book needed redoing, so I rebuilt it from scratch, from the writing to the illustrations.

Since then I make them for other houses. You can flip through that book just below.

Gustave

I try to make the book I'd love to find when I arrive somewhere: the Wi‑Fi code without having to ask, the day the bins go out, and the beach to head to when the wind picks up.

The printed book, page by page

A real 39-page landscape A4 book, written in English for its guests. For this example the house is called Casa Corallo and the hosts' details are blurred.

You get a high-resolution, print-ready PDF in A4 or A5, in your guests' language. Print it wherever you like, or I print it and ship it tracked for €30 more.

Your questions

Do you print the book?

Yes, if you like: for €30 more I print the book on thick paper with a laminated cover and send it to you by tracked parcel. You can also just get the high-resolution PDF and print it wherever you want.

Which languages?

The printed book is written in your guests' language: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish… The one shown here is in English. The phone version can hold several languages.

Do you only work in Sicily?

No. Everything happens remotely: a video call, photos, your addresses. I work with properties in France, Italy and anywhere else.

What do you need from me?

A few photos of the place, the practical info (Wi‑Fi, access, rules) and your favourite addresses. We go through the rest together on the call.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks, depending on the number of pages and languages.

What if something changes?

That's what the €30 yearly update is for: every year I refresh your book, both the printable version and the phone version.

Tell me about your house

Where it is, how many guests, where they come from. I'll reply with a proposal and a timeline. I write in English, French and Italian.
