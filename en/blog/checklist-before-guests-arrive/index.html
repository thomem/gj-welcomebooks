The checklist before guests arrive

Between two stays there's often little time. A fixed list stops you forgetting the small thing that spoils an arrival: toilet paper, an empty gas bottle, a missing welcome book.

By Gustave6 min readUpdated 24 October 2026

The house

  • Full clean, including fridge, oven and cupboards.
  • Beds made, clean towels, one per person per use.
  • Bins empty, clean, and out on the right day.
  • Windows, shutters and doors closing properly.

Supplies

  • Toilet paper, soap, washing-up liquid, sponge, bin bags.
  • Salt, pepper, oil, coffee, tea: the basics for the first evening.
  • Spare bulbs, batteries for remotes.
  • Gas bottle or wood for the barbecue if you offer one.

Safety

  • Smoke detector tested.
  • Complete first-aid kit.
  • Fire extinguisher or fire blanket in the kitchen.
  • Emergency numbers displayed and up to date. See emergency information to include.

Keep this list laminated in a locked cupboard with the cleaning products. Whoever prepares the house uses it every time.

The welcome

  • The welcome book in plain sight, in its place, in good condition.
  • Wi‑Fi code tested and matching the one in the book.
  • Air conditioning or heating set for the season.
  • A small treat if you offer one: cold water, fruit, local products.

The checklist by timing

WhenWhat
3 days beforeConfirm arrival time, check supplies
Day beforeSend the check-in message with code and address
On the day, cleaningLinen, bathroom, kitchen, bins

During the stayStay reachable, answer the first message quickly

The fix-it kit

A drawer or box that never moves, with what's most often needed: bulbs, batteries, extension lead, plug adapter for international guests, screwdriver, tape, candles and matches. Say where it is in the book.

Line for the book

For small problems, the drawer under the sink has bulbs, batteries, adapters and a torch.

Before you close the door

Walk through as a guest: come in, put a suitcase down, look for the Wi‑Fi, turn on the shower, open the book. Anything that would make you hesitate needs fixing.

Frequently asked questions

Who should use the checklist?

Whoever prepares the house, you or your cleaning team. What matters is that it's the same every time.

Should I leave a welcome treat?

It's not required, but a bottle of cold water or a few local products are usually appreciated and often mentioned in reviews.

How can I check the Wi‑Fi remotely?

Some routers let you check from an app. Otherwise, ask the cleaner to test it with their phone.
